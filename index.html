From concept to handover

Support from first idea to final handover.

Five connected stages, each with clear controls and a focus on delivery.

STAGE 01

Concept

  • Define the need
  • Test feasibility
  • Weigh up alternatives
  • Draw up master plans and roadmaps
  • Guidance on technical and statutory matters
STAGE 02

Planning

  • Prepare the plan and run studies
  • Choose equipment
  • Re-check the economics
  • Budget and cash-flow forecasts
  • Funding, programme and risk review
  • Issue the project brief
STAGE 03

Stakeholder engagement

  • Map the people and groups affected
  • Agree how and when to communicate
  • Run briefings and progress meetings
  • Gather feedback and resolve concerns
  • Keep clients, contractors and authorities aligned
STAGE 04

Monitoring

  • Set up performance tracking
  • Earned-value reporting
  • Quality assurance and checks
  • Confirm results against targets
  • Forecasts, reports and contract oversight
STAGE 05

Closing

  • Train the operating team
  • Reach full output
  • Hand over materials and clear accounts
  • Record lessons learned and close the files
  • A closing report and handover of responsibility
What we do

Specialist support across the project.

Four connected services help set direction, coordinate delivery and deal with the commercial and operational problems that come up.

01 / ADVISORY

Project consultancy

Advice on upgrading technology at a client’s site, or bringing in new systems to suit what the client needs.

  • Agree objectives and limits with the client
  • Map how planning will run, from strategy to site
  • Pinpoint the engineering outputs required
  • Shape procurement routes, contract packages and tender documents
  • Improve designs through value engineering
  • Set up programmes, planning controls, audits and good practice
02 / DELIVERY

Project management

Hands-on management from first idea to handover, backed by delivery and design experience, proven tools and sound industry practice.

  • Keep scope, quality, risk, budget, programme and resources in balance
  • Set up project controls and document procedures
  • Agree design outputs with the team
  • Build cost models and flag items with long lead times
  • Compare procurement options
  • Plan moves and occupation
  • Experience on high-rise housing and offices, infrastructure and airport work
03 / PROGRAMMES

Planning services

Turning project objectives into clear goals, work breakdowns and programmes that make the best use of the people and money available.

  • Ready-to-use planning packages
  • Weekly and monthly progress reports
  • Scenario testing and catch-up plans
  • Regular check-ins with subcontractors to chase progress
04 / COMMERCIAL

Claims support

Even well-planned projects can end up in dispute, and how that is handled shapes the result.

  • Establish what happened and why
  • Prepare and present the submission
  • Assess who is contractually responsible
  • Put a value on the impact when bringing or answering a claim
